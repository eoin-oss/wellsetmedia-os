03

Pick the feeling before the words

People remember a few of 100+ daily videos, by feeling. Brand psychology decides which feeling you pull.

Common enemy

Pulls: RESENTMENT
The thing your audience already resents. Tie your wins to beating it.
Find it
  • What do they complain about in the industry?
  • What have they tried that failed?
  • What advice are they tired of?
  • What do they feel guilty about?
Enemies saturate (anti-guru content), so it isn't enough alone.

One core belief

Pulls: VALIDATION
Pick ONE core belief and tie every piece of content to it. Hormozi: "outwork everyone".
Give "proof to collect": tie each client win back to the belief.

Consistency

Pulls: FAMILIARITY
Same look, message and behaviour, repeated until it becomes a reflex.

Polarize

Pulls: STRONG REACTION
Aim for strong agreement or strong disagreement. Those who disagree keep watching. The middle gets ignored.
Before posting ask: who am I trying to annoy, move, motivate, or relate to?

Each type pulls a different feeling. Pair it with a visual hook.

Recency

Urgency
A recent or ongoing event. A current event or trend.

Relevancy

Recognition
A fear, desire, challenge or personal story that relates a feeling. The audience's fears, desires, challenges or story.

Celebrity

Borrowed trust
Celebrity jacking. Deliver your point through a known name. Best when you lack authority yourself.

Proximity

Belonging
How close, by location. Same city or demographic.

Conflict

Tension
Controversial. Controversy causes strong emotion and replies.

Unusual

Wonder
Just weird.

06

Value that trains them to stop for you

Would someone pay $5 to watch this and feel satisfied? If not, don't post it.

Five tests

Relevant Built on the ICP's current problems, desires and situation (from data)
Unique A new angle, framing or uncommon view on a familiar topic, better explained
Clear and actionable A step-by-step they can use within 24 hours. No need to watch another video
Verifiable Backed by proof. They can see themselves doing it
Lasting impression An epiphany or strong emotion. People remember videos by feeling

Raise perceived value before you give it

Call out a specific undesired state. They don't care until they're problem-aware
Lead with a desired state you reached through your mechanism: your results
Apply your method to what an industry leader did

Insight vs value

INSIGHT, for Instagram
What you did: first we found X, then Y. Builds curiosity.
VALUE, for YouTube
Exactly how, step by step. People still buy implementation.

Package it: one idea, 200+ videos

There are only about 10 to 12 core problems. The rest is volume through formats.

Steal like an artist
Find outlier videos, well above that account's average
→
Add your twist
Change one thing: cinematic, a third clone, a new hook
→
Make it your format
When one hits, double down and iterate

Copying the top format in your niche gives its creator more authority. If others copy yours, the association returns to you.

The multiplier

Rephrase x4: same idea, new words
"Getting my dad's live reaction to my income at 19" becomes "telling my mom how much I made at 19"
Reframe x3: new angle
"Top 10 ways to stay rich" becomes "Top 10 ways to stay broke"
Reformat: new packaging (most important)
Run the same idea through your list of 5 to 10 formats

Pavlov's rule: you're training their thumb

Every rewarding video trains people to stop for you. Every empty one is a small electric shock that trains them to swipe.

Your video
Rewarding
They stop for you next time
Empty
They swipe you next time

Pre-post check

What emotion does it give me? Excited, scared, anxious from being vulnerable: all good signs
Would I pay $5 for it?
What does the viewer gain?
Conviction beats polish. The best videos are often unscripted and raw. Ask about the story and the why.
AI as drafting help. Feed it five-plus of your own videos, then edit by hand. Never post AI scripts untouched.
