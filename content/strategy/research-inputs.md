# 30-day strategy: research inputs (12 Nov 2026)

Eoin is feeding research before we lock the 30-day strategy. One entry per source.

## 1. Story sequences (Joshua Jay Agbisit, "how to create story sequences so good they beg to buy", youtu.be/WNVJ4Y4itR0)
- Content loads the gun, story sequences pull the trigger. Stories are the easiest way to reach every follower at once.
- Structure = same as a reel: hook -> amplifier -> value -> CTA (optional).
- 6 hooks: recency, relevancy (their fears/desires), celebrity jacking (borrowed authority), proximity (same city/demo), conflict, weird.
- Amplifier = the reason to keep tapping (not a second hook). 14 types; his favourites: social proof, killing expectations ("I didn't run ads, didn't post daily, I did X"), references (claim backed by proof).
- Full amplifier list (on screen at 4:07): social proof, desire, roadblock, controversial, personal story, information gap,
  call out, negative assumption ("and no, it's not X, Y or Z"), risk reversal, celebrity jacking, reference, visual,
  scarcity, killing expectations.
- Value = not just education: tools, resources, insights, step-by-steps, relatable story with a lesson, entertainment. Must cause an emotion or nobody acts.
- 4 types: documentation (no structure, just the week), wins/testimonials, value, limiting beliefs/objections. Mix them within one sequence too.
- Cadence: at least 3 a week, rotate types. Daily sequences with the same CTA = predictable = ignored. Not every one ends on a CTA.
- Proof he cites: limiting-belief sequence at 8k followers -> 50 inbounds in 30 min (his claim).

## 2. Ideation (same creator, "laziest way to create content ideas that actually attracts buyers", youtu.be/s8ymtDFmkE4)
- Make TOF for the DREAM FOLLOWER (personal traits, interests, story, goals, fears), MOF for the ICP (qualification + problems). ICP lives inside the dream-follower audience.
- Dream follower: assume first, then ask clients: what in the content pushed you to buy, what besides the value made you want to work with me, why do you enjoy working with me, what part of my brand do you resonate with most.
- MOF ideas come from qualified leads: most asked questions, pain points, which videos they watched, stage, goals.
- Idea multiplier: SLA (steal like an artist: outliers from small + big accounts in niche/adjacent) -> rephrase (same idea, said differently, x4) -> reframe (new angle, x3) -> reformat (proven formats: day-in-life, POV, live sales call, podcast...).
- "Content does everything but sell."

## Journey structure (Joshua Jay Agbisit, via Eoin 13 Nov 2026)
For personal-arc reels that aren't pure storytelling: **hook -> backstory -> epiphany / shift moment -> come up / journey -> outcome -> valuable lesson.**
Fits TOF journey reels (hollow path, the climb, long game): the dropout, DJing, Ibiza, the dream course. The lesson is the payoff; the shift moment is the turn.
