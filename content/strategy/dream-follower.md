# Eoin: dream follower + ICP (v0, 12 Nov 2026)

## ICP pain (from real sales calls + audits)
Sources: Eoin Murphy call 15 Oct + audit, Joe Quinn call 13 Oct + audit, Kyle Ritchie audit/onboarding.

1. **Their results outran their content.** The story and authority are real but never make it on camera.
   Murphy: business dropped from 100k+ to 70-80k a month and recovered, 12 of 15 SDRs at Stripe Dublin are his alumni: none of it in the content.
   Joe: "incongruency gap": coaches people on content, has no personal brand of his own.
2. **Doing it themselves burns them out.** Murphy: self-made green screen, editing burns him out, high effort, low leverage.
3. **They've been burned by hires.** Murphy: an outside creator (felt gimmicky) + an in-house hire (ideas, no execution, let go).
4. **Views don't turn into DMs.** Murphy: caption asks fell 79/132 -> 8/108 while reach rose. Joe: 0 of 289 captions ask for anything; same clip reposted 7 times in 11 min.
5. **Nobody owns the strategy.** Kyle: YT has a creative director, nobody runs Instagram; TOF thin.
6. **The money goal is DM revenue.** Murphy: ~20k -> 50-70k a month from DMs, with a setter + closer already.

Why they dipped (Eoin, 12 Nov): Murphy wanted it in-house, not an outside team. Joe: Eoin didn't book the next call on the call and gave him space to reconsider; he hasn't invested in anyone since.
Lessons: "in-house vs outside team" is a live objection = MOF seed. Never end a yes without the next call in the calendar (already the rule since 28 Oct).

## Dream follower (from Eoin, 12 Nov, his words where possible)
**Who:** ambitious, curious, 18 to 30-ish (plus older founders who still feel it). Good at school, handed a path they
didn't choose, and can feel how hollow it is. Builders: they love making things. Gym, travel, reading.
Into human behaviour and influence, science, history, philosophy, AI.
**What they want:** freedom, building something of their own, massive wealth as a long game, to see the world.
**What they fear:** working for someone else, no freedom, reaching the end and regretting what they didn't do because they were scared.
**Who they admire:** people with respectable origins who did it themselves from the bottom. "A higher inner climb."
**What they get wrong:** they put things on a pedestal and say "I can't do that". "You can't do it until you can."
They think you need to be special.
**Their tension (Eoin's too):** being smart is a curse. Overthinking while people who don't think at all go and make tonnes.
