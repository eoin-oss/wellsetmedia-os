and the mechanics behind it, so patterns show up over time. Newest first. Update in place.

## The patterns so far (Eoin's read, 11 Oct 2026)

1. **Negativity or desire.** The hook either suggests harm/danger/loss, or names a strong want.
2. **Strong claim.** Extreme, exclusive, certain: "only", "ever", "has to", "so strong it".
3. **Word choice.** One loaded word carries it: evil, obey, terrifies, identity.
4. **Text hook ≠ spoken hook.** The text sits beside the promise and adds a charge (warning, admission,
   removal). It never just summarises the reel.

Tally (how often each pattern appears in logged winners):

|---|---|
