## Stakeholder Approval Workflow

### Roles

| Role | Person | Responsibility |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Project Owner | [NAME] | Final approval on all deliverables |
| Copy Reviewer | [NAME] | Reviews for voice match, accuracy, compliance |
| Technical Reviewer | [NAME] | Reviews for platform compatibility, tracking, links |
| Subject Matter Expert | [NAME] | Reviews for factual accuracy, proof validation |

### Approval Process

**Step 1: Draft Review**
- Agent produces deliverable
- Copy Reviewer checks voice match, banned phrases, framework application
- Turnaround: [24h / 48h / 72h]

**Step 2: Client Review**
- Project Owner reviews for accuracy, tone, offer alignment
- Feedback format: [Inline comments / Loom video / Written notes]
- Turnaround: [24h / 48h / 72h]

**Step 3: Revision**
- Agent incorporates feedback
- Maximum revision rounds before escalation: [2 / 3]
- If feedback contradicts copy-foundation.md, flag and discuss before revising

**Step 4: Final Approval**
- Project Owner signs off
- Technical Reviewer confirms platform compatibility
- Status updated to APPROVED in Deliverable Checklist

**Step 5: Deployment**
- Copy loaded into platform (email tool, page builder, ad manager)
- Technical Reviewer confirms tracking is live
- Status updated to LIVE

### Feedback Rules
- All feedback must reference specific sections or lines, not general impressions
- "I don't like it" is not actionable feedback. Ask: "What specifically feels off, and what would feel right?"
- Voice-related feedback gets added to learnings.md for future sessions
- Performance data (open rates, CTR, conversion rates) gets added to learnings.md within 7 days of launch
