# Power-Words Bank (filtered through copy-rules)

> A positive vocabulary to *add* — complements `copy-editing`'s words-to-*cut* lists. Harvested from the
> AI Ads Mastery copy primer, then **filtered against `Context/Marketing/copy-rules.md`**: every banned
> AI-hype word (revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changer, seamless, robust, elevate, empower, transform
> your, unlock the power of, leverage, utilize, streamline, groundbreaking…) is **excluded by design**.

**Rule of use:** power words are seasoning, not substance. Max 1–2 per section. Never stack them
("amazing, incredible, stunning"). A specific number beats any power word — `proof-pyramid` and
specificity always win. If a sentence only works because of the power word, the claim underneath is weak.

## Trust / credibility
proven · tested · verified · documented · independent · third-party · named · on-record · guaranteed
(only if a real guarantee exists) · published · measured · audited

## Urgency / scarcity — REAL only (see offer-structuring "Legitimate Urgency")
closes · deadline · cohort · capped · last · remaining · until [date] · before [date]
> Never on a digital product with no real limit; never an evergreen reset. Fake urgency is banned.

## Value / gain
free · included · keep · own · save · faster · sooner · fewer · without · instead of · in [timeframe]

## Sensory / concrete (Cashvertising-style — earns its place when it paints a real picture)
watch · feel · hear · hold · see · picture · imagine [specific scene] · the moment [x] happens

## Action verbs (for CTAs — pair with the outcome)
get · start · claim · book · build · install · ship · launch · join · download · grab · steal (informal)
> "Get the install" / "Book the call" / "Start the build" — verb + the thing, not "click here."

## Specificity boosters (the real power)
exactly · specifically · [number] · [timeframe] · [named person/company] · step-by-step · the exact
> These do the work the hype words pretend to. "The exact 7-step install" beats "the ultimate solution."

## Banned here (would fail copy-rules — do NOT use as "power" words)
revolutionary, game-changer, cutting-edge, groundbreaking, seamless, robust, streamline, elevate,
empower, transform your, unlock the power of, leverage, utilize, skyrocket, unleash, supercharge,
next-level, world-class, best-in-class. (Specificity replaces all of these.)
