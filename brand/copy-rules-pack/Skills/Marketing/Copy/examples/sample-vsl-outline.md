# Sample VSL Outline: Apex Growth System

> Example of a complete VSL outline for a fictional high-ticket coaching offer. This demonstrates what a well-structured copywriter agent deliverable looks like. Every section follows the frameworks in SKILL.md and references the proof pyramid, headline formulas, and copy foundation.

---

## Pre-Writing Analysis

**Client:** Apex Growth Partners (fictional)
**Offer:** Apex Growth System - 12-week coaching program for B2B agency owners
**Price:** $8,500 (payment plan: 3 x $3,200)
**Guarantee:** Full refund if you don't add $30K+ in new revenue within 90 days of completing the program
**Named Mechanism:** The Pipeline Compounding Method

**Awareness Level:** 3 (Solution Aware)
The audience knows they need a better system for generating clients. They've tried ads, referrals, cold outreach. They don't know this specific product exists.
**Copy Strategy:** Lead with the desired result, hint at the mechanism, reveal it as the solution.

**Market Sophistication:** 4 (Mechanism Wars)
Agency coaching is saturated. Everyone claims a "system." The angle must prove and enlarge the mechanism, not just name one.
**Winning Angle:** Prove the mechanism with specific data. Show WHY this method works when others don't.

**Primary LF8 Desire:** #6 (Superiority / Winning)
Secondary: #5 (Comfortable Living), #3 (Freedom from Fear and Pain)

**The Conversation Already Happening:**
"I'm working 50+ hours a week and still can't predict next month's revenue. I've tried everything. Cold email stopped working. Ads are too expensive. Referrals are inconsistent. I'm good at what I do but I can't figure out the growth piece."

---

## VSL Structure

**Target Length:** 35-45 minutes
**Format:** Slide-based with voiceover (agency owner presenting)
**CTA:** Book a strategy call

---

### SECTION 1: HOOK (0:00 - 2:00)

**Goal:** Stop the scroll. Earn 60 more seconds of attention. Match the conversation in their head.

**Hook Option A (Proof-Led):**
"In the next 38 minutes, I'm going to show you the exact system that took our agency from $18,000 a month with 3 clients to $127,000 a month with 14 retainer clients in under 6 months. And I'm going to show you why everything you've been told about agency growth is making the problem worse."

**Hook Option B (Curiosity-Led):**
"If you're an agency owner doing between $10K and $40K a month and you feel like you're working harder than ever but your revenue won't budge, there's a specific reason. And it has nothing to do with your service quality, your pricing, your niche, or how many cold emails you send."

**Hook Option C (Question-Led):**
"What would change in your business if you knew, with certainty, that 15 qualified sales calls would show up on your calendar every single month without you chasing a single one of them?"

**Pattern interrupt after hook:**
"Stay with me for the next few minutes. What I'm about to walk you through has generated over $14 million in new revenue for 340 agency owners in the past 18 months. And it works whether you have a team of 2 or a team of 20."

---

### SECTION 2: PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION (2:00 - 7:00)

**Goal:** Articulate their pain better than they can. Make them feel understood. Earn trust through specificity.

**Problem Statement:**
"Here's what I know about your situation, because I lived it for three years. You're good at your craft. Your clients get results. You have testimonials. And yet every month feels like starting over."

**Pain Point Stack (3-5 specific pains, each with a vivid detail):**

1. **Revenue unpredictability:**
"You closed two clients last month. This month, your pipeline is empty. You've been in business for 2+ years and you still can't tell your spouse what next month's income will be."

2. **The founder bottleneck:**
"Every new client means more of YOUR time. You're the strategist, the account manager, and the new business person. You didn't start an agency to work 55-hour weeks doing $200/hour work."

3. **Failed growth attempts:**
"You've tried cold email. It worked for 3 months, then response rates dropped to 0.3%. You've tried running your own ads. You spent $4,000 and got 2 calls, neither of which closed. You've tried networking events and posting content daily. Referrals trickle in. Nothing compounds."

4. **The comparison trap:**
"You see other agency owners posting about their $80K months and wonder what they know that you don't. You've bought courses. You've joined masterminds. Some gave you a tactic that worked for a month. None gave you a system that works every month."

**Agitation (emotional consequence):**
"The worst part isn't the revenue. It's the feeling. That quiet anxiety on the 15th of the month when you check your pipeline and it's thin. The forced optimism when your partner asks 'how's business?' and you say 'it's getting there.' The exhaustion of being busy every day but not growing."

---

### SECTION 3: CREDIBILITY BRIDGE (7:00 - 10:00)

**Goal:** Establish the right to teach. Connect personal story to their situation. Earn the authority to present the mechanism.

**Origin story:**
"Three years ago, I was running a digital marketing agency with 4 clients doing about $22,000 a month. I was the best practitioner I knew. My client results were outstanding. And I was stuck."

**Failure sequence (what you tried that didn't work):**
"I hired a business coach who told me to 'niche down and do outreach.' I sent 12,000 cold emails over 6 months. I got on 40 calls. I closed 6 clients. I lost 4 existing ones during the same period because I was so focused on new business. Net gain: 2 clients and $14,000 in coaching fees."

**Discovery moment:**
"Then I noticed something. The two clients I closed without any outreach, the ones who came through a webinar I did and an old case study on my website, stayed longer, paid more, and referred other clients. Every client I chased had a shorter lifespan than every client who came to me."

**Realization (seeds the mechanism):**
"That's when I stopped asking 'how do I find more clients?' and started asking 'how do I build a system where qualified clients find me and arrive ready to buy?' That question changed everything."

**Current credentials (specific, not vague):**
"Since then, we've grown to $127K/month with a team of 7. More importantly, 340 agency owners have implemented this same system. Collectively, they've added over $14 million in new annual revenue. 73% of them hit an additional $30K/month within their first 120 days."

---

### SECTION 4: MECHANISM REVEAL (10:00 - 18:00)

**Goal:** Introduce the named mechanism. Explain HOW it works. Make the solution feel inevitable, logical, and different from everything else they've tried.

**Mechanism name and frame:**
"I call it the Pipeline Compounding Method. And it works on a principle that every agency owner understands but almost nobody applies to their own growth: compounding returns."

**Why existing approaches fail (enemy frame):**
"Here's why cold outreach, paid ads, and referrals keep you stuck. They're all linear. You send 100 emails, you get 3 calls. You spend $3,000 on ads, you get 5 leads. You ask for referrals, you get 1 this month. Every month, you start from zero. There's no compounding. Every client you acquire costs the same effort as the last one."

**How the mechanism works (3-step framework):**

**Step 1: Authority Asset Engine**
"First, you build 3 authority assets that work for you 24/7. Not social media posts that disappear in 24 hours. Not ads that stop the moment you stop paying. Permanent assets. A case study library structured for search. A weekly insight series that positions you as the expert. And a diagnostic tool that qualifies prospects before they ever talk to you."

"These assets take about 4 weeks to build. After that, they generate attention while you sleep."

**Step 2: Compounding Distribution**
"Second, you deploy these assets through 3 distribution channels simultaneously. Each channel feeds the others. Your case studies get quoted in your insight series. Your insight series drives traffic to your diagnostic tool. Your diagnostic tool captures leads who then see your case studies. Every piece strengthens every other piece."

"This is where compounding kicks in. Month 1, you might get 8 qualified leads. Month 2, 14. Month 3, 23. Month 4, 31. Same effort. Exponential results. Because assets compound while outreach doesn't."

**Step 3: Conversion Architecture**
"Third, you install a conversion architecture that turns those inbound leads into booked calls and closed deals without you personally chasing anyone. An automated qualification sequence. A pre-call education series that warms prospects so they arrive understanding your value. And a consultative sales framework that closes 35-40% of qualified calls."

**Mechanism proof (why it works at a deeper level):**
"The Pipeline Compounding Method works because it aligns with how B2B buyers actually make decisions. 81% of B2B buyers have already decided on a vendor before they reach out for a conversation. They researched. They read. They compared. If your authority assets are what they find during that research phase, you're not selling them on a call. You're confirming a decision they already made."

---

### SECTION 5: PROOF STACK (18:00 - 25:00)

**Goal:** Overwhelm skepticism with volume and variety. Show different people in different situations getting results.

**Anchor statistic:**
"340 agency owners. $14 million in collective new revenue. 73% hit $30K+ additional monthly revenue within 120 days."

**Case Study 1 (most relatable):**
"Meet David Chen. David runs a 3-person SEO agency in Austin. When he started, he was doing $16,000/month, working 50+ hours a week, and getting all his clients through LinkedIn DMs. Within 90 days of implementing the Pipeline Compounding Method, he had 22 qualified inbound leads per month. He closed 6. His revenue hit $54,000/month. He hired 2 people and cut his own hours to 35 a week."

"David's words: 'I went from dreading my pipeline every month to having a waitlist. That shift happened in about 60 days.'"

**Case Study 2 (different industry/size):**
"Rebecca Torres runs an 8-person web development agency in Toronto. She was stuck at $40K/month for two years. She'd tried three different agency coaching programs. Her problem was not a lack of tactics but a lack of system. After implementing the Compounding Method, she crossed $100K/month in month 4. More importantly, her close rate on inbound calls went from 18% to 41% because prospects were arriving pre-sold by her authority assets."

**Case Study 3 (overcomes a common objection):**
"James Okafor was skeptical. He told me on our strategy call that he'd spent $12,000 on agency coaching programs and none of them worked. He almost didn't join. He decided to try because of the guarantee. Within 45 days, he'd already generated $37,000 in new contracts. He sent me a message that said: 'I've made back 4x my investment and I'm only halfway through the program.'"

**Testimonial rapid-fire (4-5 quick quotes):**
- "'We went from 3 to 11 retainer clients in 5 months.' - Michael S., performance marketing agency, Chicago"
- "'The Pipeline Compounding Method replaced our entire outbound team. We saved $8,400/month in labor costs alone.' - Priya K., content agency, New York"
- "'I was ready to shut down the agency. Six months later we crossed $70K/month.' - Anna L., branding agency, London"
- "'The pre-call education sequence alone was worth the investment. Our show rate went from 55% to 89%.' - Carlos M., paid ads agency, Miami"

**Aggregate proof:**
"Across all 340 members: average time to first new client from the system: 34 days. Average revenue increase in first 120 days: $32,400/month. Average close rate on inbound calls: 37%."

### SECTION 6: OFFER PRESENTATION (25:00 - 30:00)

**Goal:** Present the offer with clear value. Stack the components. Anchor the price against alternatives.

**Transition from proof to offer:**
"Now, you've seen the method. You've seen the results. The question is whether you want to implement this in your agency. Let me show you exactly what you get."

**Core offer:**
"The Apex Growth System is a 12-week implementation program. Not a course you watch alone. A structured build-out where you implement the Pipeline Compounding Method in your business with direct support at every step."

**Value stack:**

| Component | What It Includes | Standalone Value |

| The Pipeline Compounding Method (complete system) | All 3 phases: Authority Asset Engine, Compounding Distribution, Conversion Architecture. Templates, scripts, and build guides for every element. | $6,000 |

**Price anchor:**
"Most agency coaching programs charge $15,000 to $25,000. Most marketing agencies charge $5,000 to $10,000 per month just for lead generation that stops the moment you stop paying."

**Price reveal:**
"The Apex Growth System is $8,500. Or 3 payments of $3,200."

**ROI framing:**
"The average member adds $32,400/month in new revenue within 120 days. That's a return of nearly 4x your investment in the first month of results. Every month after that is pure upside."

---

### SECTION 7: GUARANTEE (30:00 - 32:00)

**Goal:** Remove remaining risk. Make the decision feel safe.

**Guarantee statement:**
"Here's my guarantee, and I've kept it deliberately aggressive because I want you to feel the risk has shifted entirely off your shoulders."

"Implement the Pipeline Compounding Method for 90 days. Follow the weekly build sessions. Complete all 3 phases. If you haven't added at least $30,000 in new revenue to your agency by the end of those 90 days, email me. I'll refund every dollar. No complicated process. No fine print."

**Why the guarantee works (meta-framing):**
"I can make this guarantee because 73% of members hit the $30K mark within 120 days. And the ones who don't typically hit it by month 5. The method works. I'm willing to bet my revenue on your results."

---

### SECTION 8: OBJECTION HANDLING (32:00 - 37:00)

**Goal:** Address the 3-4 objections that stop people from booking the call. Handle each at the root-fear level.

**Objection 1: "I've tried coaching programs before and they didn't work."**
"I understand that concern because 62% of our members said the same thing on their strategy call. Here's what's different. Most coaching programs teach you tactics. A cold email template. A LinkedIn script. A webinar framework. Those are tactics, not systems. Tactics stop working when the market changes. Systems compound over time. The Pipeline Compounding Method is a system. David Chen had tried two coaching programs before he found us. James Okafor had tried three. They both told me the same thing: 'This is the first time someone gave me a system instead of a tactic.'"

**Objection 2: "I don't have time for another 12-week program."**
"You're running an agency. You're already at capacity. I get it. Here's the math: the program requires about 5 hours per week for 12 weeks. That's 60 hours total. The average member saves 15+ hours per month on client acquisition after implementation because inbound replaces outbound. So you invest 60 hours once and save 180+ hours in the first year. The question isn't whether you have the time. It's whether you can afford to keep spending 15 hours a month chasing clients manually for another year."

**Objection 3: "The price is steep for where I am right now."**
"If your agency is doing $10K+ per month, you're already investing time and money in growth. The question is ROI. If you're spending $2,000/month on ads that generate 3 leads and close 1 client, you're paying $24,000/year for 12 clients. The Apex Growth System costs $8,500 once and the average member adds 4-6 new clients in the first 120 days. After that, the system keeps working without additional cost. This isn't an expense. It's the last client acquisition investment you make."

**Objection 4: "How do I know this will work for my specific niche?"**
"Our 340 members span 27 different agency niches. SEO, paid ads, web development, branding, content, video production, PR, consulting. The Pipeline Compounding Method works because it's built on how B2B buyers make decisions, not on a niche-specific tactic. Your authority assets will be customized to your niche during the 3 private strategy sessions. That's exactly what those sessions are for."

---

### SECTION 9: CTA + URGENCY CLOSE (37:00 - 40:00)

**Goal:** Tell them exactly what to do next. Create legitimate urgency. End with confidence.

**CTA:**
"Here's your next step. Click the button below this video and book a strategy call with our team. The call is 30 minutes. We'll review your current pipeline, identify your biggest growth bottleneck, and map out how the Pipeline Compounding Method would apply to your specific agency. If it's a fit, we'll invite you into the program. If it's not, we'll tell you. We turn away about 30% of applicants because the program isn't right for every agency."

**Urgency (real scarcity):**
"We run the Apex Growth System in cohorts of 40 agency owners. Smaller cohorts mean better implementation support. The next cohort starts on [DATE]. We currently have [X] spots remaining. When they fill, the next opening is 8 weeks out."

**Final frame:**
"You've been running your agency long enough to know that the growth problem doesn't solve itself. More hours won't fix it. Another tactic won't fix it. You need a system that compounds. That's what we've built. 340 agency owners and $14 million in new revenue prove it works. The only question left is whether you'll be in the next cohort."

**Closing CTA:**
"Book your strategy call now. The button is right below this video."

## Post-VSL Notes

**Testing recommendations:**
1. Test Hook A (proof-led) vs Hook B (curiosity-led) first. These represent fundamentally different opening strategies.
2. If cost-per-qualified-lead is high, test a shorter version (25 minutes) that cuts Case Studies 2 and 3 and shortens the objection section to 2 objections.
3. The proof stack section is the highest-leverage section to update as new case studies come in. Rotate fresh proof monthly.

**Proof gaps identified:**
- No third-party media mentions currently available. Recommend pursuing a podcast tour or industry publication feature to add Tier 1 proof.
- Before/after screenshots would strengthen the proof stack. Ask 3-5 top members to share dashboard screenshots with permission.

**Funnel brief notes:**
- This VSL feeds into a strategy call booking page. The post-VSL confirmation page should reinforce the main case study (David Chen) and add a "what to expect on your call" section.
- Pre-call email sequence should mirror the VSL's objection handling in emails 3 and 4.
- Retargeting ads for VSL viewers who didn't book should lead with Hook C (question-led) since they've already seen the primary hook.
