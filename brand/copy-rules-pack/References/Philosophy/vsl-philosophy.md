# Our Philosophy on VSL Acquisition

## What the VSL Funnel Actually Is

A VSL (Video Sales Letter) funnel is an infinitely scalable customer acquisition system — on one condition: the sales function must be dialled in before adspend is increased.

This is not a nuance. It is the single most important truth about VSL funnels. Every other element of the funnel (creative quality, audience targeting, funnel structure) is secondary to close rate. A perfect VSL with a 10% close rate is a slower path to profit than a mediocre VSL with a 25% close rate.

---

## The VSL's Only Job

The VSL does not sell the offer. The VSL sells the call.

This distinction changes everything about how a VSL should be written, structured, and evaluated.

**What the VSL must do:**
1. Install hope — the prospect must believe that the outcome they want is achievable, through a mechanism they haven't tried before
2. Position the call as the logical next step — not a sales pitch, but a conversation where they'll learn how to get there

**What the VSL must not do:**
- Describe the full programme in detail (they'll use it to decide without booking)
- Make the outcome feel easy or certain (it creates discount expectations)
- Oversell (sophisticated markets have heard everything — overselling triggers scepticism)

**The hope installation model:**
The mechanism is not what the service does — it's *why* this approach will work when their past attempts haven't. It's the specific principle or system that makes the outcome possible. The closer the mechanism is to addressing their exact failed attempts, the stronger the hope installation.

## The Close Rate Math (Why This Is Non-Negotiable)

The relationship between close rate and economics is non-linear. A small improvement in close rate produces a large improvement in profitability.

**Example: $30,000 monthly ad spend, CPBC $200, SUR 60% = 90 live calls**

| Close Rate | Revenue (@ $5K) | Profit (after adspend) |
|------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| 10% | $45,000 | $15,000 |
| 15% | $67,500 | $37,500 |
| 20% | $90,000 | $60,000 |
| 25% | $112,500 | $82,500 |
| 30% | $135,000 | $105,000 |

Going from 10% to 25% close rate, at the same adspend, generates 5.5x more profit.

**This is why:** before adding spend, increasing offers, or testing new funnels — the close rate must be at 25%+. Below 25%, the business is not ready to scale. It is ready to fix its sales process.

## Why Most VSL Funnels Underperform

**Reason 1: Scaling before the close rate is validated**
Founders see low CPBC, feel excited, and increase adspend before confirming close rate. The unit economics collapse at scale.

**Reason 2: The VSL tries to sell the offer instead of the call**
Too much detail, too many features, too much proof — the prospect makes a decision before booking. "I'll think about it" is the most common outcome of an over-informing VSL.

**Reason 3: Wrong mechanism**
The mechanism is generic ("we build funnels for you") rather than specific and new ("we reverse-engineer your 3 highest-LTV clients to find the one message that gets more of them"). Generic mechanisms don't install hope — they confirm the prospect has already tried something similar.

**Reason 4: Misaligned expectations on the call**
The VSL sets one expectation (a free strategy session) and the closer tries to close on the call. When there's a mismatch, the prospect feels baited. The VSL and the sales process must be aligned on what the call is and what will happen on it.

## The Profitability Threshold

For a VSL funnel to be worth scaling, it must pass this threshold:

| Metric | Minimum to Scale |
|--------|-----------------|
| Close Rate | 25%+ |
| Cash ROAS | 2x |
| Revenue ROAS | 5x |
| SUR | 50%+ (B2C) / 65%+ (B2B) |
| NOI | 35%+ (target: 45%) |

If any of these are below threshold, scaling adds cost faster than it adds revenue. Fix the constraint first (see `theory-of-constraints.md`), then scale.

---

## The Mindset: Patience on Validation, Speed on Scale

The most common error: rushing to scale before validation is complete.
The correct approach:

1. **Validate the funnel at low spend** ($3K-$10K/month) until all thresholds are met
2. **Document what's working** — which creatives, which audiences, which funnel structure
3. **Scale with structure** — increase spend on what's validated; don't change variables during a scale push
4. **Monitor closely at each new spend level** — what works at $5K may not work at $30K without adjustments

Speed matters after validation. Before validation, speed causes expensive mistakes.
