description: Never write "we don't guarantee views/followers/revenue" in client-facing proposals or plans
---
Don't put a "we don't guarantee views, followers, calls or revenue" line in client-facing docs. Eoin, 15 Oct 2026, on the Eoin Murphy plan: "I dont but dont say it goddamn."

**Why:** it plants doubt in a sales doc; the no-guarantee point belongs in the contract, not the pitch.
**How to apply:** keep it out of proposals, plans and Looms; still never promise a number (feedback_copy_rules_pack).
