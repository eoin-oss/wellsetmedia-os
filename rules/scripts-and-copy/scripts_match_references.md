When he hands over reference reels, write in their shape: him talking it through like a person (hook, what it is, what he types and what comes back, why it matters, CTA naming the thing), about 100 to 140 words for 30 to 45 s.
**Why:** 20 Oct 2026 the editor-launch scripts came out as short fragments that described the graphics ("This is paper. Every card cut out…", "Coffee. Phone. Gym.") instead of the Jason Cooperson / migs.visuals talk-through he asked for. He called them shit and binned the whole set, costing him the studio session.
**How to apply:** before writing, transcribe the references and match their word count and rhythm; write 2 samples and get a yes before batching; never let rulebook constraints (no foil, short lines) flatten the voice into captions. Related: feedback-creative-output-is-his, project-content-system-offer.

**Hooks rule (20 Oct):** every editor-launch hook says straight away that AI edited THIS video ("I edited this entire video using Claude Code", "This entire video is edited by AI", "This video was edited by something that isn't even human"). Curiosity comes from that claim. Vague hooks like "I filmed this once" are out. Bodies then open on a line that follows any of them.
