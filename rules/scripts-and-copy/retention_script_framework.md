Every personal-brand video goes through Eoin's 5-phase retention framework (source: Notion → Learning Lab → Notes → "Structuring videos for retention") AND must loop. Locked 2026-09-19 on the first script, FORTUNE.

**The phases, as we apply them**
1. **Hook (0-3s)** opens a question the viewer needs closed. Mid-action picture, voice from frame one, never a static frame, never a quote or branding.
2. **Context** = who / where / why it matters in ONE working line. No self-intro (user-age-no-self-intro).
3. **Conflict** is the tension, NOT the realisation. The realisation is the payoff; if it's used as the conflict the middle goes flat. Use his real self-talk/words. End the conflict on a hinge line that sets up the payoff. Shot change every 2-5s.
4. **Turn + Payoff** closes the exact contract the hook opened. Tension releases here: music to near silence, angle change, shots held twice as long.
5. **Echo** = the line that sticks. Eoin's ruling 2026-09-30: it does NOT have to loop. A loop (final line cut mid-sentence into the hook, same shot) is one option; the other is a line or a single question for the viewer that lands after the payoff. Pick per video.

**Rules:** first person throughout, max one "you" line (a closing viewer question counts as that one). B-roll mirrors the arc (handheld/tight before the turn, tripod/wide/still after). On-screen text under 8 words a line. No CTA on tape, caption only. Record echo+hook as one sentence so the audio join is inaudible. Run the comment test first: what should people type under it.

**Why:** his last videos had none of this and underperformed; he wants it used every time and to understand each stage himself.

**How to apply:** work ONE video at a time in conversation — narrative line, then each phase, then full script, then shot list grouped by setup. Do not batch-write scripts or push to Notion until he says lock it. Locked scripts go in Notion → Marketing Hub → Content Creation → Content Pipeline (data source 76978866-2485-8228-92ac-8702273bcf55). Videos must serve the brand narrative at `$WSM_HOME/internal/brand-narrative.md` (theme: the desire for more).
