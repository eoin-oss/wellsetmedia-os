**It does not replace project_retention_script_framework — it sits underneath it.** The short-form
map (setup → conflict → resolution → emotional echo) maps 1:1 onto hook/context/conflict/turn+payoff/echo.

**What it genuinely adds:**
1. **The midpoint: want → need.** The story turns when the character stops chasing what they wanted
   and finds what they needed. Usually a failure or an awakening — a truth that recontextualises
   everything before it. Pinpoint it *before* writing any scene.
2. **Define the lingering emotion first** — what's still in the room after the last frame.
3. **The one-sentence test:** summarise each act in one sentence. If you can't, the structure isn't
   finished. Run before writing.
4. **External resolution alone leaves the audience empty** — the internal change is the ending.
5. **Circular story** = ends where it began, perspective changed. This is why his loop works, and
   the reason to keep using it.
6. **Hooks: curiosity, not confusion** — these fail in opposite directions. Establish emotional tone
   immediately; hint at the central question without answering it.
