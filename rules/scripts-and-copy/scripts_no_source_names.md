---
name: feedback-scripts-no-source-names
description: Scripts never name or quote unknown people or outlets from research; the research supports his point
---

Personal-brand scripts must not name or quote the people or outlets behind the research (journalists, academics, execs, blogs) unless his audience already knows them. Well-known brands are fine as examples. Turn the evidence into his own words and develop his point.

**Why:** 25 Oct 2026, on a Think Tank draft that quoted a Lush brand director and named the outlet: "it cant be referencing these random guys off of some blog post if no one knows who it is. keep the names out and just develop my point."

**How to apply:** rule 6 in `$WSM_HOME/content/scripts/SCRIPT-RULES.md` and in the Think Tank draft prompt (`hq/engine/newsroom.py`). Applies to every script, not only Think Tank drafts. Related: feedback_script_anti_generic, project-personal-brand-thoughts.
