Before any motion-graphic still goes to Eoin, review it critically and fix it first. Don't send something just because it rendered.

**Why:** 8 Nov, Kyle's method graphic. After about 8 rounds he asked "are you actually reviewing this and saying, yeah, that's cool?"
- He said it looked "cheap as fuck" and "super ai".
- The faults were all ones I could have caught myself: a tiny title, the most basic font, pill text sitting off-centre, Success not in the middle and not standing out, empty space, and lines running through the text.

**How to apply:** check every frame for these before sending.
- **Hierarchy:** a big title in the brand heading style (Garamond, second word in gold italic).
- **Type:** brand type with character. Plain Helvetica is not enough on its own.
- **Centring:** measure the label centring from the rendered pixels.
- **Hero element:** the hero sits dead centre and dominates.
- **Space:** the frame is filled without clutter.
- **Lines:** no line crosses any text.
- **Colour:** gold reads as gold, not washed out by glow.

When stuck on a look, pull real references first. Working from patterns that already exist is what unlocked this. Related: feedback-mograph-stills-first, feedback-check-text-boxes.
