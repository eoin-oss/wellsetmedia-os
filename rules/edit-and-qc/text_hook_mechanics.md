Eoin, 2026-10-11: "i always say this - notice the mechanics of it." On "(DO NOT USE FOR EVIL)" (Tristan's text hook, worked twice: 45k and 36k views): it suggests negativity, it creates curiosity, and the word choice is strong. Text hooks are imperative for Kyle's short form.

**Why:** he keeps having to point out the mechanism himself. Copying a hook word for word, or just summarising the reel, misses why it works.
**How to apply:** when he shares a reference reel, name the mechanics first: a warning or a sense of danger, an open question, charged words, and text that sits beside the spoken hook rather than repeating it. Then write variants in the client's own world. Text hooks warn, take something away, or admit something; they never summarise the reel. Never reuse a prospect's signature line verbatim (Tristan is a live lead). Related: feedback_script_anti_generic, project_kyle_ritchie_lead.

**Log:** every flagged hook goes into `$WSM_HOME/content/HOOK-PATTERNS.md` (numbers + mechanics + pattern tally), newest first, so Eoin can see the patterns. His four: negativity or desire, strong claim, word choice, text beside the spoken hook rather than repeating it.
