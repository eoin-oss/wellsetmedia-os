The July Fathom clip (jay_jul.mp4) opens on Kyle in his car for ~5.5s before cutting to Jay, and the card played from frame 0. A Jay-only cut (jay_jul_jayonly.mp4) already existed and was used in 03, but not in the case study. Stills QC checked text/layout, never the identity of the person in the footage.

**Why:** Eoin, furious: "how did you fuck that up". Showing the coach as the client in a client-proof reel is a credibility-killing error on a live post.

**How to apply:** Fathom/call clips switch speaker views. Before using any call clip or photo as "person X", pull a contact sheet of the exact source range used and confirm X is the one on screen for every frame. Prefer the pre-cut single-person files (`*_jayonly`, `*_hold`). Do the same in QC stills: name who is in each labelled card. Related: feedback-check-text-boxes
