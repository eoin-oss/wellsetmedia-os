QC happens at least **1 hour after the edit, with a different task in between**, so the video is forgotten and he comes back fresh. Never QC straight after editing. Every reel still gets the phone watch-through even when the machine checks (preflight, safe zone, music guard, run automatically by `render_tls.py`) pass.

**Why:** October's Kyle misses (14 logged, three went live) happened because QC was done lazily, straight after the edit. Eoin, 28 Oct: "QC is never done right after editing, its done with time inbetween so perspectives are fresh."

**How to apply:** when I finish a render or batch, I say it's ready for QC after a break. I never mark it QC'd or schedule it in the same sitting. SOP `$WSM_HOME/sops/07-qc.md`. Related: sop-from-kyle-work.
