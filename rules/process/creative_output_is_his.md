**Claude gathers, structures and systematises. Eoin creates.** Content ideas, scripts, a month's
content plan, format definitions and shooting SOPs are his output, not Claude's — even when he has
just described exactly what they should contain.

**What went wrong (2026-10-01):** the task was to pull data out of Kyle Ritchie's Notion. Claude
pulled it, then kept going — wrote a source-intel doc, an onboarding prep doc, started a month of
content, and started shooting SOPs. He stopped it twice: *"dont go and make the months worth of
content yourself. that comes from me"*, then *"again stop making shit by yourself. thats stuff I need
to do. have you got the data from the notion or what thats the original task."*

**Why:** he's the creative director — the judgement and the eye are the product he sells. A plan
Claude wrote is a plan he has to audit line by line, which is slower than writing it himself and
lands outside his taste. It also pushes past the actual ask while the original question goes
unanswered.

**The tell:** when he describes a deliverable in detail ("50% TOF, tier lists, Books in 60 seconds,
headroom for text above his head"), that is him thinking out loud and setting his own brief — **not
a spec to go execute.** Answer the question that was asked, note the brief, and stop.

**How to apply:** finish the task that was actually set and report. Supply raw material, research,
intel, structure, access lists, measurement, systems and automation — the things that save him time.
Offer the creative build in one line and wait for a yes. Related:
feedback_never_create_unapproved_entries, project_morelife_kyle_cd.
