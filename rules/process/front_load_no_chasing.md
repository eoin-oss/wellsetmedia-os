Eoin (2 Nov 2026, planning Kyle's November): "I WANT THIS MONTH TO RUN ALMOST AS IF WE NEVER EVEN NEEDED TO TALK TO EACHOTHER." Block out the tasks up front instead of chasing resources and waiting.

**Why:** chasing clients for assets mid-month stalls delivery and makes him look disorganised; the shoot and edit must never wait on the client.

**How to apply:** every client month starts with ONE call + ONE handover list: decisions (each with a default if the client doesn't decide), answers said on a recorded call (transcript = script source), documents with a 48h deadline, photos into one folder, and access (scheduler, ManyChat, Insights, footage). Approvals = weekly batch, 48h silence = approved. Anything not in by the deadline runs on its default. Template: `clients/kyle-ritchie/november-pack/next-call-prep.md` §0.
