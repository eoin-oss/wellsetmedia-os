name: feedback-batch-shooting-by-setup
description: Eoin shoots content batched by location/setup across many scripts at once, not script by script — shot lists must be organised setup-first
---

Eoin's production method (his words, 2026-09-21): **one shoot day, batched by location.** He'll sit in a single spot and read the middle line of eight different scripts back to back — it makes no sense until post, and it saves enormous time. Shot intentionality plus ruthless pre-production is the point.

**Why:** the bottleneck isn't filming, it's set-up and travel. Collapsing eight scripts into three or four setups is the difference between a day and a week.

**How to apply:** never hand him a shot list organised script-by-script (A/B/C/D per reel). Organise **setup-first** — one master list grouped by location and lighting state, with every shot tagged to the script(s) it serves and the line it covers. Per-script pages keep the narrative, rules and the edit order; the shoot document is a different artefact.

**The governing rule: all shots must be intentional.** Every frame in a batch day names the script and the line it serves before it's shot. If it can't, it doesn't get shot — that's what stops batching from producing generic b-roll, which is its only real failure mode.

Outfit continuity is **not** a constraint he cares about: most of what's batched is b-roll serving the narrative of several reels, so looks are mixed anyway. Don't raise it again. Related: project_wsm_content_formats.
