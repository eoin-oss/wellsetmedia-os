# 10 · Month end

**Trigger:** the last week of the client's month.

## Steps
1. Pull the six numbers against the month-1 baseline: followers, average views, profile visits,
   inbound DMs, calls booked, posts shipped.
2. Top 3 and bottom 3 reels: what they had in common. Log each format's result in the outlier sheet (03b).
3. One-page report (template: `_archive-2026-10/05-report-strategy-session.md`).
4. **Call (30 min):** the numbers, what worked, what changed in their world, one thing to stop, one to do more.
5. Renewal or upsell on the call. Next shoot date locked before it ends.
6. **Internal retro (Eoin):** what ate time, what slipped, what worked. One page in `../reviews/YYYY-MM-retro.md`.
7. Update these SOPs and the client's rules file from the retro.

**Done when:** report sent, call done, next shoot date set, retro written, SOPs updated.

## Rules
- The baseline is pulled once in month 1 and never re-pulled.
- Same six numbers every month. No extras.
- Kept light for now. The full version comes with the joint offer.
