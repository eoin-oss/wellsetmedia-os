# 07 · Quality control

**Trigger:** a batch is exported (06), and **at least 1 hour has passed with a different task in between.**
Never QC in the same sitting as the edit. Come back with fresh eyes.

## Steps
1. Open the `qc-report.md` the render wrote (or run `tools/auto-editor/engine/preflight.py "<folder>"`). Fix any FAIL.
2. Have the client's `CONTENT-RULES.md` open.
3. **Phone, full screen, sound on, start to finish, no scrubbing.** Every reel. Then the checks below.
4. Verdict per reel in the report: **PASS** / **FIX** (timestamp + what) / **BIN** (one line why).
5. A FIX gets fixed in Resolve and re-exported, and the **re-export goes through 07 again from the top**.
6. PASS only moves on to approval (08).

**Watch for:** jumps in the face · clipped first or last word · mid-sentence joins · stumbles left in ·
hook there, 2 lines, readable in 1s · captions match the words, never over the face, inside the
safe zone · music allowed for this client · both audio channels · no flashing in the first 2s · grade
matches · client rules (topics, swears, names) · price never said or shown · CTA keyword matches
the caption.

**Done when:** every reel in the batch is PASS or BIN in `qc-report.md`.

## Rules
- Anything posting within 48h that hasn't passed comes out of the scheduler. A late post costs nothing.
- Live and wrong: archive (never delete), fix, re-QC, repost in a later slot, add a line to the misses log.
- Every miss becomes a "watch for" item so it can't slip twice.
