# 08 · Approval

**Trigger:** reels marked PASS in QC (07). The client only ever sees reels that passed.

## Steps
1. Send the PASS reels to the client's approver (named in their `CONTENT-RULES.md`) in one batch,
   each with its hook and caption.
2. Ask for one reply per reel: **approved** or **notes**.
3. Notes = the one revision round. Fix in Resolve, re-run 07, send back as v2.
4. After v2 the reel is closed. Later notes roll into next month.
5. Chase once if there's no answer after 48h.

**Done when:** every reel has an explicit "approved" from the client or their approver.

## Rules
- **Nothing posts without an explicit approval.** Silence is not approval.
- Read notes literally. If a note is ambiguous, ask before acting.
- Fix vs new brief: trims, cuts, caption edits, music swaps are the round. A new angle or new footage is a new brief.

The review tool is per client for now. A standard review system comes with the joint offer.
