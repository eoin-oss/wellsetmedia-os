# 04 · Pre-production

**Trigger:** the shoot date is set (03 or 10) and the month's formats are picked (03b).

## Steps
1. Plan the month's slots from the formats picked in 03b: the funnel mix (reach, objection-killers, case studies), spread across the
   week, one per series per week, proof the day after a claim.
2. Eoin writes the scripts. Facts only from what the client has said; a missing detail is `[NEED]`, never invented.
3. Hooks written with the script, never after. Stacked reversals, loop closed at the end.
4. Scripts in the shape of the reference reels: 2 samples first, then the batch.
5. **Script review slot, in the calendar, at least 2 days before the shoot.** Read each one out loud. Fix or cut.
6. Shot list by setup across all scripts, every shot naming the line it serves.
7. Footage pulls for clips from existing material: read the whole transcript, select on full sentences.

**Done when:** every script is locked and reviewed and the shot list is by setup, 2 days before the shoot.

## Rules
- **Scripts ready on time is non-negotiable.** The shoot never moves for a script.
- Copy rules apply to every script (`../brand/COPY-RULES.md`, `../content/scripts/SCRIPT-RULES.md`).
- Claude does research and structure. The ideas and the words are Eoin's.
