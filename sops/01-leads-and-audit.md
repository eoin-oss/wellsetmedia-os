# 01 · Leads + audit

**Trigger:** a new name, from outreach, a referral or a warm contact.

## Steps
1. **ICP check before anything else.** Mindset coach, hypnotist or hypnotherapist, or transformation
   work. Selling a high-ticket offer off their own face. Behaviour filters only, never origin.
2. Fails the ICP: no audit. A warm exception gets a non-flagship offer at most, and is flagged as an exception.
3. Passes: run the full audit with `/content-audit` (the template is locked, never redesigned per client).
4. Read the killer finding back against the numbers before it goes anywhere.
5. Any outreach copy written before the audit gets rewritten from the audit's verdict.
6. Send the audit as the opener for the call (Loom + link). Nothing is sent without Eoin seeing it.

**Done when:** the lead passed the ICP check, the audit is built and checked, and the call is asked for.

## Rules
- The audit is the closer. Who gets one is the decision, not whether to do one.
- Never name another account in an audit or opener.
- Dead leads are written down as dead and never chased.
