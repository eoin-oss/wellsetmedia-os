# 03b · Research and iteration

**Trigger:** onboarding done (03) for month one, then every month end (10).

## Steps
1. **Keywords.** 10 to 20 for the client's niche: their offer, their audience's problems, the words
   their audience uses (from the questionnaire and their comments).
2. **Outlier sheet.** Search every keyword on IG, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Log every video at
   **500k+ views** to `clients/<slug>/research/outliers.csv`: link, account, followers, views, date, keyword.
3. **Stage.** Tag each one **grow (TOF), nurture (MOF) or sell (BOF)**.
4. **Why it worked.** Name the main driver, then the rest: the idea, the hook, the format,
   the quality, the lighting, the edit and pacing, the person, the emotion. One column each.
5. **Month one is an experiment.** Copy the winning formats into the month plan across grow,
   nurture and sell. Every video names the format it copies and its stage.
6. **Month two.** Repeat what worked in month one (top performers from 10) and bring in new
   formats from fresh research as tests.
7. **Every month after:** repeat what works, test new ideas, in all three stages. Log each test's
   result against the format in the sheet.

**Done when:** the sheet has outliers tagged by stage with the why filled in, and every video in
the month plan maps to a format and a stage.

## Rules
- Copy the format, never the content. The ideas and the words are still Eoin's (04).
- Every month has tests in grow, nurture and sell. Never a month of repeats only.
- A format stays on the client's numbers against their own median, not on how it did for someone else.
