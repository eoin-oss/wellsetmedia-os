# 02 · Sales call + close

**Trigger:** a call is booked.

## Steps
1. On booking, send the **pre-call questionnaire**. [NEED: link to Eoin's questionnaire]
2. Night before: a Chase Hughes profile (`chase-hughes-profiler` agent) and the 2-page pre-call sheet
   (BEFORE: profile, how they buy, claim → proof → scenario · DURING: flow, audit, objections, packages).
3. **Discovery:** their problem and goal in their words, what's been tried, who's on their team,
   budget, who else decides. Pitch the shape and a price on the call.
4. **Plan within 24h:** a custom plan page + PDF they can show a partner. The system, the month,
   getting started, their time vs ours, price line by line, scope, terms, next step.
5. Send: a Loom walking the plan + the PDF + one relevant sample.
6. **Close: on a yes, book the onboarding call there and then.** The date goes in both calendars
   before hanging up. Never wait on a form or a reply.

**Done when:** the onboarding call is in both calendars.

## Rules
- Custom offers are fine. They closed the month. Never under the floor without a written reason.
- Price lives in the plan and the call, never in content.
- Reference builds: `clients/eoin-murphy/proposal/plan.html`, `clients/*/call/pre-call-sheet.pdf`.
