# 03 · Onboarding

**Status: being rebuilt as a client hub (Vercel) from Eoin's onboarding call recording.** Until then this is the minimum.
The full old call flow is in `_archive-2026-10/08-onboarding-call.md`.

**Trigger:** the onboarding call booked at the close (02 step 6).

## Steps
1. **Contract and payment on the call.** No payment, no work: no scripts, no shoot dates.
2. Deep story questionnaire filled straight after payment (`client-docs/story-interview.md`), so prep starts that day.
3. **Fill the client rules file** at `clients/<slug>/CONTENT-RULES.md` from the template: banned music,
   banned topics, swearing, people never to name, slots, approver, hook format.
4. Assets collected (logos, fonts, past footage, testimonials, case-study photos).
5. Shoot dates and the billing day locked before the call ends.

**Done when:** paid, the rules file is filled, the questionnaire is in, the first shoot date is set.

## Rules
- Every client rule found later in the month gets added to their rules file the same day.
- The rules file is read before every edit (06) and QC (07).
- [NEED: questionnaire timing, decided in the rebuild. Current default: pre-call questionnaire before the sales call, story questionnaire after payment]
