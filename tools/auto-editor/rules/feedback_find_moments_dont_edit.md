When he asks to transcribe and find the strong moments, the deliverable is the list of moments. Nothing gets cut,
captioned or rendered until he says how. Edits happen in DaVinci Resolve, done his particular way. Never render
outside Resolve with ffmpeg or burned-in captions.

**Why:** 18 Oct 2026, Hempton x Galligan podcast. He asked me to "go through it and come back to me". I built an ffmpeg
reframe and caption pipeline and started rendering 30 clips. He stopped it: "i didnt say to edit it ... I want this
done in davinci in a particular way."

**How to apply:** "Come back to me / let me know what you can do" means report and wait. Mentions of captions,
crop or face tracking describe the end product. They are not the go-ahead to build it. Ask how he wants it done in
Resolve before touching the edit. Related: [[project-auto-editor]], [[project-hempton-galligan-clips]].
