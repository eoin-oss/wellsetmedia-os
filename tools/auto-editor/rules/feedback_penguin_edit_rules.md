Eoin's review of "the polymaths win" stills (11 Nov 2026), his own content in the @longevitypenguin style:
- **White text only, never mixed black and white in one shot.** On his blue bedroom wall, captions are white. Black only on the off-white canvas.
- **No tilted cards.** Canvas cards sit straight, like the refs.
- **Minimal SFX.** No caption click or shutter sounds ("really bad and non minimal"). Later tightened further: no whooshes or hits at all, only quiet ticks plus a riser and a flash on the strobe.
- **He judges motion, not just layout.** "i dont see no motion at all": a static build reads as unfinished, so show the animated version once the stills are close.

**Why:** his taste for the penguin look is clean and minimal. The measured auto black/white colour choice broke it.
**How to apply:** builder `$WSM_HOME/tools/auto-editor/jobs/glasgow-bodies/build.py` (pick() is white-only and only decides the halo). Refs and breakdown are in `research/penguin-edit-refs/`. Related: [[project-penguin-caption-system]], [[feedback-mograph-stills-first]].

**Later notes, same day (now in MODE.md):**
- Cut every pause, measured against his speech level, not digital silence.
- Zooms are slow and never fully stop.
- Lists are black/white word cards cut on each word.
- Captions go on every talking shot.
- Quiet ticks only, plus a riser from frame 1 and a flash on the strobe.
- Never the Loki song.
- No 1-frame gaps or stray frames.

**11 Nov, after posting (77% skip rate):** no text hook in the penguin style ("its whats said. and whats seen"). Fix = bigger, better captions in the opener, and a spoken hook line with a promise or curiosity gap. The SOP-11 text-hook rule does not apply to penguin edits.

**12 Nov:** opener inset = quick blur-in (1s max) + ramped zoom-out to no less than 80% (46% was too small/fast/not subtle). Style is a BASE, vary the devices per video ("i dont want the style to be the exact same every single time"). **Never render until he says so**: show stills / timeline instead.
**12 Nov (content got boring):** opener words a beat each, shots breathe; payoff can zoom OUT slowly (BORING on the hands-over-face C2354); list words can sit on matching b-roll instead of cards; cutaway pushes start on frame 1 and never stop; captions vary per line (chest / raised-left wall / stacked lead + big word / Didot Italic accent), bigger, never on the face.
