## The skeleton (DeT0W1GRZSe)
- **One A-roll angle** the whole way: seated side-profile on a bench, sunlit, wide, lots of empty sky/space on the left.
- **Cutaways = him, alone, in big places** (back to camera at a window, at a river, in a train station, on a rooftop). Every payoff line moves to a new place. The A-roll carries the connecting words.
- **Open = micro-montage:** 4 shots at 3–4 frames each, the caption building "ONE / ONE OF THE / ONE OF THE BEST" across them. Then the flash. Then the A-roll.

## Flashes
- **Invert strobe (DeT, 0.5s):** frames 18 and 20 are an inverted (negative) frame, with a normal frame 19 between them. It's 3 frames, at the hand-off from the montage to the A-roll. A second, softer one sits at 27.7s.
- **White blow-out (DfkJ, 1.3s):** exposure ramps up over 2 frames into 1 to 2 pure-white frames, then fades back down into the next shot over about 3 frames. Used as a transition into a new section.
- **White flash frames on beats (DfM5, DfHbO):** 1 to 2 frames at near-white (luma 190–230) on section changes, roughly every 8 to 12s.

## Zooms
- **Slow push in** on held cutaways (DfkJ "CREATOR" shot, the river): about 3–5% over the shot, eased.
- **Punch-in cut** on the A-roll between sentences (DfPYW, DfkJ): same take, about 10–15% tighter, hard cut. It hides the jump cut and resets attention.

## Text, white to black (the key rule)
- **Text colour follows the background.** Light sky or wall behind the words means near-black/charcoal text (DeT A-roll: "SKILLS", "LEARNED"). Dark or busy plates mean white.
- **Caption = one word at a time**, Helvetica Neue Bold caps, small (about 4% of frame height), placed in the empty space (left of his head on the A-roll), never on the face.
- **Payoff build on cutaways:** a small lead-in typed word by word ("NOBODY", "OH EVERYONE IS") → the **big payoff word** (about 18% of frame height, tight tracking) **behind him** (he occludes the letters: CA[head]ES, LOO[body]KING, REA[body]IZE) → small trailing words appear under the right edge ("ABOUT YOU", "AT ME").
- **Single colour accent:** DeT uses navy for one payoff ("LIVES"). DfM5 and DfHbO run orange as the brand accent.

## Canvas backgrounds (DfM5, DfHbO)
- A **solid canvas** (white #f5f3f0 or burnt orange #d9541e) with the footage shrunk onto it as **framed photos/cards**: polaroid-style stills, tilted screenshots of the IG profile, a vertical phone clip, side by side.
- **A cutout of him on the white canvas** with big orange words behind him, and diagrams/numbers (01 02 03) built around him.
- Used for the "proof" moments (follower counts, the profile, receipts) and section openers. 2 to 4s each.
