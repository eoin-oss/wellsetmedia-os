Pairs with editorial

Everything looks cut, torn and taped by hand. He becomes a cut-out photo on a paper stack, key words arrive as ransom-note scraps, and transitions are pages tearing away. Stop-motion jitter at 12fps on the graphics only, so the talking stays smooth.

Templates
Torn-edge reveal, taped photo, ransom word, paper stack transition, cut-out sticker of him, scribbled arrow
Type
Mixed per word: Bodoni, Anton, typewriter, italic Playfair
Sound
Paper rustle, tear, tape rip, scissors snip, stamp
Pacing
Medium. Graphics on the key nouns, two or three per sentence
Best for
Story reels, opinions, anything personal
