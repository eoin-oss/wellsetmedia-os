# Kallaway format breakdown (19 Oct 2026)

Source: 4 reels in `reference/kallaway/` (DY2BBsIuFcC story arc, DZU1D_8OI62 Claude analyze, DeW4HF2uL3P dopamine loop,
DcqiQesO_R9 scriptwriting format). Contact sheets at 2fps + 12fps transition strips alongside.

## Layout (9:16)
- Split mode (~80% of runtime): graphics stage top ~60%, his WIDE shot in a rounded window across the bottom
  (~y 62-92%, small side margin). The window is cropped tight so his head sits at the top edge, and a cutout of his
  head, cap and hands breaks out above the window edge. Same camera every time.
- Full-screen mode: a second, tighter camera (chest-up close-up) fills 9:16 for 2-5s between graphic sections, roughly
  every 8-12s. No graphics on these, caption only. Used on asides and emphatic lines.
- Caption: ONE word at a time, white bold sans ~60px, centred in the gap between stage and window (in split) or mid
  frame (full screen). Occasional word in italic serif.

## Graphics
- Two grounds: black with soft glow (story/psych topics) or light grey #e8e8e6 (UI/tool topics). One per reel.
- Title block top of stage: bold sans + italic serif mix ("Traditional *Story Arc*", "How to make your *Storytelling
  addictive*"), key word in red, or a red label bar with white text ("No Longer Works").
- Objects: line charts with glowing strokes + white dots + small boxed labels; phone/UI mockups; 3D-ish icons (brain,
  slot machine, poker chips); document with coloured bars; grids of thumbnails.
- Accent: one strong colour (red, magenta glow) + white. Glow on every stroke.

## Motion (the bit to nail)
- Nothing hard-cuts. Each graphic BUILDS (stroke draws on, dots pop on in sequence, labels appear on the word), then
  EVOLVES into the next: the same object moves, scales, re-colours or spawns the next (infinity draws on -> brain rises
  out of it on a stem -> slides aside for a slot machine; arc chart -> new curve drawn over it -> morphs to modern arc).
- Between sections: speed-ramp moves. Hard ease in-out (fast middle, slow ends) with directional motion blur, often a
  push-through (scale an element up past camera to reveal the next) or a fast slide.
- Title text: words fade/blur in one at a time, grey -> white as he says them.
- Every reveal is on the spoken word. Something changes every 0.5-1.5s.
- SFX: whoosh on every ramp, soft pops/ticks on builds, quiet music bed.
