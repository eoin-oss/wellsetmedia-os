# Style pack backlog (19 Oct 2026)

Style packs to bake into the editor (roadmap phase 1). Each pack = caption rules + pacing/zoom + graphics templates + sound
design + music rules. Built natively in Resolve (Fusion). Creator names are internal labels only; public names are descriptive.
Test every pack on the resonance raw footage and check stills.
Every pack must show the real thing when he points at it ("here is…", named posts, people or tools): pulled images, screenshots or the actual reel, framed in that pack's style.

## First batch (talking head only, no b-roll)
1. Paper cut-out: torn edges, cut-out photos of him and objects, tape, stop-motion jitter, paper texture.
2. Editorial / newspaper: headline + pull-quote layouts, halftone cut-outs, serif type, newsprint texture. Pairs with paper cut. (Eoin likes)
3. Infographic / data: counters, bar and line charts drawing on, stat cards, labelled diagrams.
4. Kinetic type: full-screen type slamming, stacking, scaling, masking to the beat.
5. Tech HUD / interface: thin lines, corner brackets, scanning, readouts, terminal type-on. The techy-minimal one.
6. Notes / app UI: iPhone Notes, iMessage bubbles, tweets, Google searches, browser windows.
7. Bold word captions: big 1-2 word captions, colour pops, punch-ins (Hormozi lane).
8. Film / analogue: grain, halation, gate weave, light leaks, warm grade, letterbox.
