## The rules it runs on

1. **Something changes every ~1.5s, but it's not always b-roll.** The changes come from: b-roll, a full-screen graphic, a
   punch-in, and overlays on his face. B-roll is only ~30% of runtime. The longest face stretch (16-23s) is carried by
   the icon list building beside him.
2. **Every visual is the line, literally.** Map for "across the world", a counter for "20 revisions", the timeline for
   "removes ums", an icon per listed item. Nothing is mood filler.
3. **Visuals land on the word.** Cuts sit 0.02-0.2s **before** the word they illustrate ("You" 5.04 → cut 4.94;
   "We" 12.48 → cut 12.28; "You" 14.26 → cut 14.24). Graphic beats animate on the spoken word.
4. **Face open is short:** 2.4s, exactly the hook sentence, then the first cutaway.
5. **Text is sparse and quiet.** Only key phrases get text, not every line. A small all-caps kicker over a larger
   regular-weight word, light grey/white, no outline, no shadow, no colour. It sits beside or behind the head, never
   over the face.
6. **Face shots vary in scale.** Punch-ins (with motion blur on the move) and slow push-ins, so a long face stretch
   never sits still.
7. **Transitions are motion, not effects.** Hard cuts just before words. Section changes get a whip or zoom with
   motion blur.
8. **One grade, dark and consistent.** The open uses the grade itself as the reveal.
9. **Audio is calm:** -15 LUFS integrated, music low, the voice carries it.
